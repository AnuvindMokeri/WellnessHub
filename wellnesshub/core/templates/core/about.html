About the project

A centralized home for wellness commerce

WellnessHub is a multi-vendor e-commerce platform built for skincare, fitness, herbal and personal-care products. It connects independent wellness vendors with customers through a single, secure, role-based marketplace — replacing scattered social-media selling with proper inventory, order and payment management.

The platform has three roles: Admin, who approves vendors and oversees the marketplace; Vendor, who lists and manages products and fulfils orders; and User, who browses, orders, tracks and reviews products.
